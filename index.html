01 — About

I know how to build from scratch.

For the past three years, I’ve worked as both a creative strategist and direct-response copywriter. Depending on the team and the project, that has meant researching audiences, developing offers, writing ads and scripts, analyzing performance, and turning what we learn into new creative directions.

I’ve worked across SaaS, health & wellness, and business opportunities, alongside creators, experts, and marketing teams across Brazil, Latin America, Europe, and North America.

I’m particularly interested in projects where there’s something to build — whether that means developing the positioning and offer for a new brand, finding the right creative angles, or building the systems and messaging that help a brand grow from its early stages.

"Working across markets has also made me more conscious of the differences between audiences. What works in one market doesn’t automatically work in another — even when the product, offer, and language are the same." I like getting close to the problem before getting close to the copy.

STRATEGY BRIEFING — MEN'S HEALTH / SUPPLEMENT
Building a VSL from a market brief, not a hunch
M.U.P/F/S
Mechanism Framework
Context

A men's-health supplement offer that needed a full strategic foundation before a single line of script got written — market, mechanism, and big idea, worked out as a standalone brief.

Approach

Mapped the target market and its existing beliefs, then built the offer's mechanism in three layers — problem, function, and solution — so the "why this works" had an internal logic, not just a claim. From there, developed the big idea and a bank of paradoxical questions to open the story on, and worked every likely objection into the offer structure (guarantee, pricing, bonuses) before writing a word of the VSL itself.

Deliverable

A full strategy document — market analysis, mechanism design, objections-to-benefits mapping, offer stack, and lead angles — used as the blueprint for the VSL script that followed.
