03 — Selected Work
SaaS · Faith & Prayer
When the ad is only part of the problem, fix the journey.
$60K/mo average ad spend · +$2M revenue in 12 months · LA Agency
2.1xwinning creative ROAS

Context

A WhatsApp extension sending daily prayers on subscription — an idea I developed alongside the CEO, built and scaled together from day one. I owned the full creative strategy.

Diagnosis

Early ads weren't getting traction. CPM was high, CVR was low, and the account was spending roughly $20K/month at a 1.6–1.7 ROAS. The combination of underperforming creative and a low-converting landing page pointed to a broader funnel problem. There was significant room to optimize across the customer journey.

Action

01 — Change the angle

Back-and-forth format · “Hurry Prayer” angle

2.1 ROAS
$27,134 spend
02 — Change the persona

Demonstrative format · “Skeptical” persona

1.9 ROAS
$15,342 spend

Beyond the ads

Sales-page optimization reduced drop-off and lifted CVR. Backend offers pushed LTV from $320 to $410. Subscription recovery and an affiliate referral loop added compounding growth.

Result

Scaled from $20K to $63K monthly ad spend, peaking at $100K in the best month. At departure, accumulated ad spend had reached $800K and the brand was still running.

Men's Health · VSL Optimization
Find the leak before you write another ad.
Sky Hunter · affiliate funnel · US / Europe
0.7% → 1.7%CVR

Context

I had just joined Sky Hunter as the main copywriter. The men's-health affiliate funnel was a mess — poor VSL metrics, heavy traffic drop-off at the beginning of the video, and low CVR.

Diagnosis

I trace funnel leakage by asking one question: what part of the funnel do people actually see?

Where does the customer stop?
Creative Pre-sell Sales Page VSL

The ads were already strong and heavily tested. So instead of writing another ad, I looked at the next funnel step — and there was no pre-sell page in use.

Action

I rebuilt the sales page from a white-page VSL layout into an organic “online news” format. More people kept moving into the video.

Then came the bigger change: the micro-lead — a 1–5 minute piece before the VSL, designed like the hook of an ad, but for the VSL itself.

Pitch retention ↑CVR grew from 0.7% to 1.7% — the funnel became scalable again.

BizOp / MMO · Hydra
Research did most of the writing.
$75K peak single-day spend · $1M+/mo at scale
$200Krevenue in one day at peak

Context

Joined Hydra to launch two new offers after two existing funnels had run flat for 10 months.

Action

Deep-mapped the niche's existing audience — the YouTube channels, Instagram profiles, and Reddit communities they already followed — plus what hadn't worked in competing offers.

The first batch hit immediately, with one ad peaking at $75K in a single day. A second offer then exposed a deeper constraint: audience overlap with US competitors was pushing CPM up.

A new persona set, combined with proven formats and angles, unlocked another tier of scale.

Result

$88K in total ad spend in a single day, driving $200K in revenue that day alone, with the account running near $1M/month at peak.
